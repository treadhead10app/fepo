Welcome back for the 22nd year of this contest! The rules have been the same every year, essentially you need to pick 6 games every week, and there are no mulligans or grace for forgetting to pick games. A missed pick counts as a loss.

The contest has 3 levels, 50-200-500, but in order to play in a higher level, you must also enter the one below it, so cost to players is 50-250-750. We generally have about 60 players for 50, 20 players for 200, and 6-8 players for 500.

Payment is due prior to selections week 1, which are games starting Sept 3/Labor Day Weekend

You can submit your payment one of 3 ways, please include your Player Name in the memo but nothing else:

  • Paypal - treadhead10@hotmail.com - Please use the friends setting
  • Venmo - @treadhead10 - Please use the private and friends setting and do not mention the contest in the memo
  • Zelle - treahead10@hotmail.com - Please use only your Player Name and do not mention the contest in the memo
